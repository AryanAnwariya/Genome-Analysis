LIMITATIONS
-----------
The prototype contains only 100000 contact records
from one chromosome pair.

It is not a genome-wide contact store.

Raw counts are used without INTERKR normalization.

TOPK results apply only to this imported subset.

No clustering or graph embeddings are required.

Large source data and the binary hictile store
remain on HPC scratch storage.
